About the village

JONNADA Village sits in a farming region where wheat, pulses and vegetables are the main crops. The Gram Panchayat office coordinates local governance, and this portal was built so residents can find scheme details, school and clinic information, and important numbers without waiting in line at the office.

Everything here is kept current by the Panchayat office. If a detail looks out of date, contact the office using the details at the bottom of this page.
